This folder contains the full KiCad projects for the 3 custom PCB designs used in the Balafon Transcription System: accelerometer boards, breakout boards, and the Arduino Due shield. The zipped folders have everything needed to reproduce the custom hardware, including KiCad schematics and 3D models, Gerber files, and bills of materials.

["balafon-accelerometer"](balafon-accelerometer-kicad.zip) and ["balafon-breakout"](balafon-breakout-kicad.zip) PCBs designed by Andrew McPherson </br>
["balafon-due-shield"](balafon-due-shield-kicad.zip) PCB designed by Katelyn Hadley
